Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Cookerize app (a recipe notebook) uses, why, and how to have it deleted.

In short.

  • Without an account, nothing leaves your device. The app works entirely offline: your recipes, your photos and your lists stay on your phone.
  • No trackers, no analytics. The app contains no behaviour-analysis tool, no social network, and no advertising profiling done by us.
  • Advertising is provided by Google (AdMob), and purchases by Google Play. These two services have their own rules, recalled below.
  • Your data is never sold.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is the publisher of Cookerize, reachable at cookerizeproject@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The app stores on your phone: your recipes, your photos, your stock, your plan, your shopping list, your preferences (units, language, theme) and your food exclusions.

This data belongs to you. It stays on your device as long as you do not delete it, and it disappears if you uninstall the app. If you do not open an account, that is all: nothing is transmitted.

3. If you create an account

The account serves a single purpose: to find your recipes on another device. What is then transmitted to our server:

DataWhyDetail
Your email address to identify you, to log you back in —
A derivative of your password to verify your password Your password itself never leaves your device: the computation is done on the phone, and the server receives only a result that cannot be reversed.
The content of your notebook backup and synchronisation recipes, preferences, stock, plan, shopping list
The photos you upload to find them on your other devices —
The device identifier to manage the connected sessions only one active session at a time

This data is hosted on our behalf by Cloudflare. It serves no purpose other than running the app: no profiling, no resale, no targeted advertising built from your recipes.

The server also keeps a non-reversible fingerprint of the IP address and browser, recorded at each sign-in attempt. It is used only to block repeated password attempts. It identifies no one, and it is never used for anything else.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays ads provided by Google AdMob. The choice of ad is made by Google's advertising network, not by us.

To display and measure an ad, that network collects for its own account:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • your IP address, which is also used to estimate an approximate location (country, city);
  • your interactions with the app (launches, taps, video views);
  • diagnostic information about how the app runs (startup time, crashes, energy use);
  • device and account identifiers related to the device, including the advertising identifier.

This data goes to Google and its advertising partners, not to us. We receive neither your profile, nor your interests, nor the reason an ad was chosen.

Your consent is requested before any personalised advertising. You can withdraw it at any time by resetting or disabling your advertising identifier in your phone's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

The subscription removes the ads: that processing then stops entirely.

  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Purchases (Google Play)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. The subscription is paid: there is no free-trial period. We neither see nor store any banking data: Google only passes us the confirmation that a subscription is active, its plan and its expiry date, and the price shown by the store prevails. Google Play's policy applies to these transactions.

6. Retention period

  • Without an account: no data with us.
  • With an account: your data is kept as long as the account exists. If you delete your account, it is erased.
  • Account left silent: after twelve months without signing in and without an active subscription (five years if you have already paid), the data attached to your account is not erased: it is archived — kept, but no longer used. It is permanently erased two years after that archiving. Your notebook stays on your phone: when you come back, the app uploads it again. A record of any payment is kept — it is what allows us to answer you.
  • Sessions: login tokens are stored in hashed form — a leak of the database would yield no usable session.

7. Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account.

Without going through the app: write to cookerizeproject@gmail.com from your account's email address, or use the page Delete my account.

Deletion is final and takes effect immediately: it erases your account, your synchronised notebook and the sessions of your devices.

What is kept, and why: the photos you upload are stored in deduplicated form — the same image is kept only once, for all the notebooks that reference it. It is no longer linked to your account, but erasing the image would remove the photo from other users. Write to us if you request its removal.

Deleting the app from your phone does not erase a synchronised account: that is precisely what it is for.

8. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data. To exercise it, write to cookerizeproject@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and knowingly collects no data about them.

10. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its update date. Important changes will be flagged in the app.
