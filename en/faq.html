Frequent questions

What we are asked most often. Short answers, and a plain “no” when the answer is no — you would rather know what you are getting into.

Understanding the app

Where do the recipes come from?

From classic cooking, gathered and rewritten one by one: quantities, steps, times. We copy no protected text, and we do not publish our sources.

Are the quantities reliable?

They come from an average of published sources, adjusted for a real dish. A family recipe may differ: it is a fair base, not a single truth.

Are the calories accurate?

No, and no app can be: they are averages drawn from published tables. Even laboratory measurement gives the energy a food contains, not what your body takes from it.

Why do some recipes have no photo?

Because a recipe without a photo beats a photo that does not show the dish. The catalogue holds more than four hundred, each photographed for itself.

Does the app need the internet?

Not to cook: your recipes live on your phone. The internet is only for your account, the subscription and the ads.

Free and subscription

What can I do for free?

Everything: create, edit, plan, shop, cook. The free tier is limited in the number of personal recipes and foods, never in features.

What exactly does the subscription change?

More recipe slots each month, the automatic pantry, batch cooking up to ten recipes, and detailed nutrition. And no advertising at all: the daily extra is granted in one tap, without a single video.

Why an ad for a bonus?

It is the only ad in the app: a video, never forced, that adds one slot. The rest of the time there is none.

How do I unsubscribe?

From the store that took the payment, in your list of subscriptions. We cannot do it for you — on purpose: the move stays yours.

Your data

Does my data leave my phone?

Without an account, no: everything stays on the device. With an account, only your recipes, pantry, shopping list and planner are synced, so you find them on another device.

What happens if I delete my account?

What is on our servers is erased, and the app becomes local again. Deletion is done inside the app, and the dedicated page describes the exact path.

Can I back up my recipes?

An account is enough: that is the backup. There is no file to manage, and nothing is lost when you change phones.

Writing to us

I found a mistake in a recipe.

Write to us: a wrong quantity or a missing step is fixed quickly, and the fix helps everyone.

I have an idea, or a bug to report.

Write to us too. A clear idea, with the gesture that led to it, beats a long list.
