Frequent questions

Short answers, and a plain “no” when the answer is no.

Understanding the app

Where do the recipes come from?

They are gathered, then rewritten one by one: quantities, steps, times. No protected text is copied, and the sources are not published.

Are the quantities reliable?

They come from published sources, adjusted for a real dish. A family recipe may differ: it is a fair base, not a single truth.

Are the calories accurate?

No, and no app can be. They are averages from published tables: a good guide, not a verdict.

Why do some recipes have no photo?

A recipe without a photo is better than a photo that does not show the dish. More than four hundred recipes are illustrated, each one for itself.

Does the app need the internet?

Not to cook. The internet is only used for the account, the subscription and advertising.

Free and subscription

What can I do for free?

Everything: create, edit, plan, shop, cook. The free tier is limited in number, never in features: 20 personal recipes to start, and no personal foods.

How much is the subscription?

€3.99 per month, or €24.99 per year. There is no free trial.

What does the subscription add?

Extra recipe slots for each month paid (they accumulate), the automatic pantry, batch cooking up to ten recipes (two on the free tier), and detailed nutrition. And no advertising at all: the daily extra is granted in one tap, with no video.

How do I unsubscribe?

In the store, Google Play, from your list of subscriptions. The store takes the payment, so we cannot do it for you.

Advertising

What is the reward video?

A video you choose to watch, never imposed. In exchange, it adds one recipe slot for the day, and one personal food slot, up to five a day.

Where else do ads appear?

Two short ads, on a free account only, at a natural pause: after you save a recipe, and after you generate a batch menu. Never while you browse, never before a batch, never to unlock anything.

Does a subscriber see ads?

No, none: neither video nor short ad.

Does advertising use my personal data?

No. No personal data is requested for advertising, and the app stores no profile and no targeting history.

Your data

Does my data leave my phone?

Without an account, no: everything is local. With an account, four things are synced so you find them on another device: your recipes, pantry, shopping list and planner.

Does the app send me e-mails?

No. Your address is only your login. The key to your account is the recovery code, shown when you create it and displayed again from the settings.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the app, or from a dedicated public page online, without reinstalling anything. What is on the servers is erased.

Can I export my recipes to a file?

No, there is no export file. The account is the backup.
