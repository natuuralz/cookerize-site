Your recipes, finally in one place

Cookerize is a recipe notebook that works in your pocket, with no network and no account. Create your own in seconds, see what you can cook with what is in your fridge, and let the cooking plan keep time for you.

What it does, and why it is different

Create a recipe without a fight

It is the number-one action, and everything is built around it. Type your ingredients: the usual quantities are suggested, the units follow your country, and the steps are built from what you actually wrote. Three ways in: from scratch, from a list, or by duplicating an existing recipe.

451 illustrated recipes, ready to cook

A catalogue of classic and world recipes — 134 of them come from 36 countries — each with its photo and its playable steps. You add your own alongside them, or you start from one of ours.

“What can I cook?”

Say what is in your fridge, and Cookerize ranks the recipes by what you can really make: first the ones that are complete, then the ones that are missing the fewest things.

The shopping list by aisle

It is derived from your plan, sorted by store aisle, and when you tick a line the ingredient goes into the fridge. You never tick the same thing twice.

The cooking plan that keeps time

Cooking three dishes at once? Cookerize orders the steps so that everything comes out hot, groups the shared actions, schedules the preheating and sets the timers. It is the trickiest thing in cooking, and it is where the app is most useful.

Offline, no account

The app works entirely without a network: your recipes are on your phone. No account is needed to use it. The account only exists if you want to find your notebook on another device.

Your units, your language

grams or ounces, Celsius or Fahrenheit: the app asks for your country on first launch and sticks to it, everywhere. Conversions are made to be usable in the kitchen, not to display pointless precision.

And for subscribers

Search by nutritional values: “less than 400 calories, more than 20 g of protein”, with two-handle sliders like a budget. And ad removal.
