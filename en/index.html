What it does

Your recipe in a fraction of a second

Tap an ingredient: it arrives already weighed for the number of servings. Name the utensil — pan, oven, casserole — and the steps write themselves in order: wash, chop, brown, add. You correct, you no longer write.

The fridge that fills itself after the shop

Tick a line on your list, and the ingredient walks itself into your fridge. Nothing to type twice.

What can I cook?

Tell it what's left: the app suggests the complete recipes first, then those missing the least.

Batch cooking, with YOUR recipes

Most apps offer their own ready-made recipes. Here you put yours in: a personal recipe fits perfectly into a session, shared actions grouped and timers set.

Grandma's bolognese: how many calories, really?

Calories, protein, fat, carbs, fibre and sugars: worked out from the ingredients of your own recipes — grandma's or the one you make every Wednesday — plus your week's daily average.

Over 1,100 recipes, offline, no account

A whole notebook in your pocket, no signal needed and nothing to create to get started.

Also: search by season — seasonality follows the region you live in —, the foods of your country brought forward, grams or ounces, Celsius or Fahrenheit, three languages — and for subscribers, detailed nutrition, with no advertising at all.

Going further: cooking with what is left · batch cooking with your own recipes · writing a recipe you can follow · seasonal food according to your region.

Cookerize at a glance

What is it? A cooking app for Android that answers the evening question: "what's for dinner?"

Who is it for? Anyone cooking at home, alone or with family, who wants to decide quickly — with what they already have.

Which problem? Deciding the meal, avoiding buying again what you already own, and letting nothing spoil at the back of the fridge.

What it does, precisely: write a recipe from its ingredients, the steps built according to the chosen utensil and the quantities following the number of servings; keep the fridge, sorted by aisle; suggest first what you can cook without going back to the shop; move what you tick on the shopping list straight into the fridge; gather several recipes — your own included — into a single batch cooking session; search by ingredient or by season; bring forward the foods of your country; work out the calories, protein, fat, carbs, fibre and sugars of a recipe, and the week's average.

What it does not do: no account required to get started, no data leaving your phone without an account, no tracker or audience measurement inside the app, no recipe export file, no advertising at all for a subscriber.

How it works: offline, with no account to get started, in English, French and Spanish.
