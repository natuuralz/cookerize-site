What it does

Over 400 illustrated recipes

Classics and dishes from around the world, each with its photo and playable steps.

Create a recipe in 30 seconds

Usual amounts suggested, your country's units, steps built from what you write.

What can I cook?

Tell it what's in the fridge: first the complete recipes, then those missing the least.

The cooking plan

Three dishes at once? Steps ordered, shared actions grouped, timers set.

The shopping list by aisle

Built from your plan, sorted by aisle. Tick a line and the ingredient goes to the fridge.

Offline, no account

Everything stays on your phone. An account only lets you find your notebook elsewhere.

Also: grams or ounces, Celsius or Fahrenheit, three languages — and for subscribers, detailed nutrition, with no advertising at all.

Free or subscriber?

You can try Cookerize without an account and without paying anything. Here is exactly what each version gives you — no more, no less.

Free

0 €

  • 20 recipe slots to start with.
  • +1 slot each day by watching a rewarded ad (one a day), up to 2,000 slots.
  • Batch cooking sessions of 2 recipes at most.
  • Pantry kept by hand.
  • 5 personal foods to start with, +1 per rewarded ad (up to 5 a day), up to 1,000.

Subscriber

3.99 € / month · 24.99 € / year

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • Your daily slot in one tap, with nothing to watch.
  • +5 slots per paid month (+70 for an annual payment), added to the same counter.
  • Batch cooking sessions up to 10 recipes (instead of 2).
  • +5 personal foods per paid month (+70 for an annual payment), up to 1,000.
  • Detailed nutrition.
  • Automatic pantry.

The same counter for everyone, and it never goes down. The slots you earn stay yours. One exception only: a refunded purchase removes exactly the slots it had granted.

No free trial. The subscription is paid, and both subscribing and paying go through the store: the app never sees any bank data. The price shown by the store is the one that applies.
