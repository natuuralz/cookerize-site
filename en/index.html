What's for dinner? Cookerize answers first

Cookerize is a cooking app: it answers the evening question with what you already have at home. Write your recipe, and the steps write themselves. Come back from the shop, and the fridge fills itself with one tap. Over 1,100 illustrated recipes are already waiting, and it all works offline, with no account required.

What it does

Your recipe in a few taps

Write the ingredients, and Cookerize builds the steps for you: your recipe is ready to follow in the kitchen before the water's even boiled.

The fridge that fills itself after the shop

Tick a line on your list, and the ingredient walks itself into your fridge. Nothing to type twice.

What can I cook?

Tell it what's left: the app suggests the complete recipes first, then those missing the least.

Batch cooking, with YOUR recipes

A Sunday spent cooking for the week? Gather your own recipes into one plan: shared actions grouped, timers set.

Grandma's bolognese: how many calories, really?

Even on a recipe you wrote yourself, Cookerize works out calories, protein and fat — and your week's daily average.

Over 1,100 recipes, offline, no account

A whole notebook in your pocket, no signal needed and nothing to create to get started.

Also: grams or ounces, Celsius or Fahrenheit, three languages — and for subscribers, detailed nutrition, with no advertising at all.

Free or subscriber?

You can try Cookerize without an account and without paying anything. Here is exactly what each version gives you — no more, no less.

Free

0 €

  • 20 recipe slots to start with.
  • +1 slot each day by watching a rewarded ad (one a day), up to 2,000 slots.
  • Batch cooking sessions of 2 recipes at most.
  • Pantry kept by hand.
  • 5 personal foods to start with, +1 per rewarded ad (up to 5 a day), up to 1,000.

Subscriber

3.99 € / month · 24.99 € / year

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • Your daily slot in one tap.
  • +5 slots per paid month (+70 for an annual payment), added to the same counter.
  • Batch cooking sessions up to 10 recipes (instead of 2).
  • +5 personal foods per paid month (+70 for an annual payment), up to 1,000.
  • Detailed nutrition.
  • Automatic pantry.

Your slots add up, and they stay yours.

No free trial. The subscription is paid, and both subscribing and paying go through the store: the app never sees any bank data. The price shown by the store is the one that applies.
