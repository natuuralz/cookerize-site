Delete my account

If you have never created an account in Cookerize, there is nothing to delete: the app works without an account, and your recipes live only on your phone. Uninstall it, or clear its data from the Android settings, and everything disappears.

If you have created an account, it contains your email address, your derived password, your synchronised notebook and the photos you uploaded. Here is how to erase everything.

Delete only part of your data, without deleting your account

You can erase a specific item without deleting your account: a recipe, a photo, a pantry batch, a planned meal or a shopping-list line. You delete it from the app, as usual, and the deletion propagates to your synced notebook: the item does not reappear on another device.

Only the elements that define the account are removed along with it: your email address, your derived password and the account record.

1. From the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and final.

2. By email

Write to cookerizeproject@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Deletion of my Cookerize account”.

It is this correspondence that proves the request really comes from you: that is the only reason we ask for it to be sent from the address concerned.

What is erased

  • Your account and your email address;
  • Your synchronised notebook: recipes, preferences, stock, plan, shopping list;
  • The photos you uploaded to the server;
  • The sessions opened on your devices.

What is not erased

  • What remained on your phone. The app does not erase your local data: if you want to start from scratch on that device, use the erase button in the settings, or uninstall the app.
  • The traces of your purchases, kept by Google. Subscriptions are sold by Google Play, which keeps its own billing histories. We have no access to them and cannot erase them for you. An ongoing subscription is cancelled from the Play Store.

Timeframe

Deletion is processed as soon as possible, and no later than 30 days. You receive a confirmation by email.

You do not need an account to use Cookerize. The account is only used to find your notebook on another device. You can delete it and keep using the app exactly as before: your recipes on this device stay where they are.
